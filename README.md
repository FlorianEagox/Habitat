A social habit tracker for CUITES ONLY

This is a nuxt app I built to learn GQL and Nuxt 3

Make an account and add all the things you'd like to spend your life doing, and then track how much you do them. You can then share your progress with friends who sign up too! We are motivated our reward-centers, maybe a concrete and data-driven way to view ourselves can  help tap into them.
